Format width:height. Leave empty to let the camera decide.
⚠️ Below roughly 790 px of output height the camera falls back to a mode that is cropped to the centre — the picture then comes out zoomed and pixellated. The full-frame modes are 1640:1232 and 3280:2464; forcing one of them lifts that limit, which matters when a sensor region is combined with a reduced height.
Leave both empty for automatic exposure. Setting them fixes it, which removes the settling time and allows a much shorter delay below. A longer exposure brightens the picture but blurs moving hens; a higher gain brightens it too but adds noise.
⚠️ Unlike white balance, the right exposure depends on ambient light, which changes between midday and dusk even with the lamp on. Watch the pictures for a few days before settling on a value.
The delay is the time given to auto-exposure to settle before the shutter fires. Too short and the picture uses provisional gains — often too red.
AI inference endpoint

URL of the local LLM used by the "AI analysis" button. Leave empty to mark the feature as not configured.

Turning this off stops everything at once: the analysis button on this page, the analysis endpoint, and the automatic morning and evening door check. No request reaches the inference server.
AI timeouts & retry

HTTP timeouts and retry policy used by the "AI analysis" button. Only connection failures are retried (timeouts mid-inference are not, to avoid multi-minute waits). Changes take effect on the next reboot.

When a request fails, it is retried after a pause that doubles each time — 2 s, then 4 s, then 8 s — up to the longest wait. This gives a struggling server room to recover instead of hammering it. Worst case before giving up: attempts × read timeout, plus the pauses.
